Flood & Water-Level Monitoring System

1 • Problem Statement & Solution Overview

Problem Statement:

  • Floods occur due to heavy rainfall, overflow of rivers/dams, and poor drainage systems. Most areas lack real-time monitoring.

Solution Overview:

  • Real-time monitoring using sensors in rivers, lakes, dams, and drainage channels.
  • Store continuous readings for historical analysis.
  • Track rainfall and predict rising water levels; send alerts to authorities and citizens.
  • Sort and analyze water-level trends to detect high-risk areas.
  • Simulate water spread using graph traversal algorithms (BFS) for preparedness.

3 • Time & Space Efficiency Analysis

  • Arrays: Efficient for historical data; O(1) access, O(n) insertion.
  • Queue: Ideal for live sensor data; O(1) enqueue/dequeue.
  • Max-Heap: Quickly identifies highest water level; insert O(log n), max O(1).
  • BFS: Simulates water spread; time O(V+E), space O(V).
  • Hash Table: Fast sensor lookup; average O(1).
  • Selection Sort: Trend analysis; simple but O(n²), not for large datasets.
