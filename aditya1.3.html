Flood & Water-Level Monitoring System

1 • Problem Statement & Solution Overview

Problem Statement:

  • Floods due to heavy rainfall, overflow of rivers/dams, poor drainage systems.
  • Real-time monitoring lacking in most areas.
  • Difficulty predicting water-level rise and sending alerts.
  • Historical water-level data not properly stored.
  • Limited simulation of water spread to identify high-risk zones.

Solution Overview:

  • Real-time monitoring using sensors in rivers, lakes, dams, and drainage channels.
  • Store continuous readings for historical analysis.
  • Track rainfall and predict rising water levels; send alerts to authorities and citizens.
  • Sort and analyze water-level trends to detect high-risk areas.
  • Simulate water spread using graph traversal algorithms (BFS).

3 • Time & Space Efficiency Analysis

  • Array: O(1) access, O(n) insertion, Space O(n)
  • Queue: O(1) enqueue/dequeue, Space O(n)
  • Max-Heap: O(log n) insert, O(1) max, Space O(n)
  • BFS: Time O(V + E), Space O(V)
  • Hash Table: Average O(1), Space O(n)
  • Selection Sort: Time O(n²), Space O(1)
  • Total Time Complexity: O(V + E + n²)
  • Total Space Complexity: O(max(n, V))
