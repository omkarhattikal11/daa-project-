Climate-Resilient City Resource Management System

1 • Problem Statement

  • Traffic congestion during emergencies (floods, heatwaves)
  • Unequal distribution of critical resources (water, energy, medical aid)
  • Inefficient waste and resource routing
  • Overloaded infrastructure during peak demand

2 • System Design

  • City represented as a graph (nodes = zones, edges = roads with weights)
  • Dynamic data models climate impact (road blockage, demand surge)
  • Modules: Emergency Routing, Resource Distribution, Waste & Utility Optimization, Decision Support

3 • Algorithms & Data Structures

6 • Principles & Generalization

  • Applicable to Smart traffic systems, Disaster management, Smart grids, Healthcare logistics, Waste recycling networks
  • Algorithms remain the same; only input data changes

7 • Transfer of Knowledge

  • Graph algorithms → Transportation & evacuation
  • Priority queues → Resource triage
  • Trees → Infrastructure management
  • DP & Backtracking → Planning under constraints

8 • SDG-11 Alignment

  • Reduces congestion
  • Improves emergency response
  • Promotes efficient resource usage
  • Enhances urban resilience to climate risks
