Bank & Government Queue Management System

1 • Problem Statement & Proposed Solution

Problem:

  • Long wait times due to inefficient queue handling.
  • Priority handling needed for VIPs, senior citizens, and emergencies.
  • Customers require routing to multiple service counters.
  • Dynamic queue changes as some customers leave or switch queues.
  • Tracking serviced customers and average waiting time is difficult.

Solution:

  • Regular customers in FIFO queues, VIPs in priority queues.
  • Multiple counters simulated with arrays tracking current counter time.
  • Assign customers to least busy counter for efficiency.
  • Maximum service time calculated to monitor efficiency.
